About YJSS

युवा जनकल्याण सेवा संस्थान (YUVA JANKALYAAN SEVA SANSTHA) is a diverse collective of individuals who have overcome humble beginnings to become lawyers, teachers, engineers, and more. Our organization is fueled by a commitment to transform lives and uplift communities.

We believe in the power of education, unity, and empowerment to create lasting change. From rural villages to urban centers, we work tirelessly to bring opportunity and hope to every home.

Our Mission →

Our Mission

We are dedicated to creating a positive and lasting impact on individuals and communities through education, empowerment, and harmony.

Education for All

Providing education to every home and child, regardless of socio‑economic backgrounds.

Libraries in Villages

Establishing libraries in every village to empower individuals with access to information.

Women Empowerment

Prioritizing women's education and creating opportunities for them to thrive in every field.

Drug‑Free Society

Creating an educated culture and promoting a drug‑free society for a healthier future.

Harmony & Brotherhood

Fostering unity among diverse communities and maintaining social harmony.

Modern Agriculture

Motivating rural farmers towards modern, sustainable farming practices.
